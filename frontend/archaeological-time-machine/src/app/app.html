CORE PEDAGOGY

What is a "Temporal Horizon" in Archaeological Science?

Standard history textbooks often present the past as isolated vertical timelines (e.g., India in isolation, Rome in isolation, Egypt in isolation). A Temporal Horizon does the opposite: it takes a horizontal cross-section across the entire planet at one frozen year.

01
Contemporaneity: Discover which settlements, kings, and craftsmen coexisted on Earth at the exact same moment.
02
Trade Networks: Trace direct cargo exchanges (such as Indus carnelian and seals found in Mesopotamian Ur).
03
Parallel Innovation: Compare how different civilizations solved water management, writing, and metallurgy in the same era.

Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) — Research, Antiquity & Field Activities

Under the Antiquities and Art Treasures Act, 1972 (Act No. 52 of 1972), an "Antiquity" includes any coin, sculpture, painting, epigraph or other work of art or craftsmanship that has been in existence for not less than one hundred years. The ASI directs systematic scientific excavations, documentation of stratified finds, and preservation in specialized in situ site museums across India.
